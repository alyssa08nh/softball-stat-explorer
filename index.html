A data story

The Rise of the
Power Hitter

Home runs are changing the shape of college softball offense. Here is what that shift means for the modern lineup.

01 / CONTEXT

For decades, small ball defined the college softball playbook: move runners, manufacture pressure, and make every at-bat count. That approach still matters, but the offensive center of gravity is moving.

02 / DATA ANALYSIS

The trend below places home runs per game beside sacrifice bunts per game. As power production climbs, sacrifice bunting steadily recedes, signaling a game that is rewarding slugging depth and longer at-bats.

03 / CONCLUSION

A deeper power profile gives teams more ways to turn a baserunner into a run. The modern roster is built for both: speed and contact at the top, with reliable extra-base threat throughout the order.
